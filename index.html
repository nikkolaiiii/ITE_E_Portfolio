A reflection on the ideas and skills developed throughout the course.

NY
01

What I learned

I strengthened my skills in organizing and analyzing data, using spreadsheet formulas, creating charts, and writing basic Python solutions.

02

What challenged me

Some statistical concepts and programming tasks took time to understand, especially when several steps had to work together.

03

How I worked through it

I reviewed examples, broke each task into smaller steps, practiced, and checked my results so I could learn from mistakes.

04

How I will use it

I plan to apply these skills in future classes and projects to organize information, solve problems, and explain results clearly.

COURSE LEARNING SUMMARY

This course gave me practical experience with data, Excel, and Python. It also helped me become more patient, methodical, and confident when learning new technology.
